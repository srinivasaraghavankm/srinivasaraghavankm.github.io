About

I lead Voice AI technology development at Navana Tech, building India's voice AI — speech technology that understands the country contextually, not just linguistically, across telephony noise, accents, dialects, code-switching, and low-literacy users.

As Head of AI at Navana Tech, I lead the development of the company's Voice AI stack powering AI contact-centre intelligence for BFSI enterprises — voice agents deployed for loan collections, loan sales, and customer support that hold up under real-world telephony conditions across India.

Over nearly seven years at Navana, I've built a multidisciplinary AI team from the ground up — speech scientists, ML engineers, computational linguists, and language specialists — and led the in-house Bodhi Speech API: a compute-efficient, low-latency, real-time streaming ASR platform for 10+ Indian languages and 40+ dialects. Optimised for CPU deployment, it delivers production-grade performance at low total cost of ownership across several BFSI enterprises.

I've worked in automatic speech recognition for over a decade — beginning at the SPIRE Lab, IISc Bangalore, then an MS by Research at IIIT Bangalore with a thesis on low-resource ASR. My work centres on speech and language technology for low-resource languages, breaking language barriers, and expanding digital access for linguistically diverse populations.

I represented Navana's leadership in RESPIN — as data partner alongside IISc and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation — building one of the first large-scale open-source dialectal speech corpora for Indian languages: 10,000+ hours across nine languages and 35+ dialects. The RESPIN-S1.0 corpus was accepted at NeurIPS 2025, and I've authored 10+ publications at NeurIPS, Interspeech, and ASRU.
